Most people get blood results as a page of numbers and flags, and ten minutes with a GP to make sense of them.

So I built BloodAI.

Upload a lab report and it:
→ reads it from a PDF or a photo
→ checks every value with fixed rules, not AI guesses
→ compares you with ~17,000 adults your age and sex (models trained on US NHANES data)
→ explains it all in plain language, and talks you through it by voice

It never diagnoses. It helps you walk into your GP appointment with the right questions.

Project #11 this year. See how it works: gasanbuildsai.vercel.app/work/bloodai
